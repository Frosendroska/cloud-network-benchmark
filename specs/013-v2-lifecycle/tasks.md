## Dependencies and execution strategy

T001 →T002–004 →US1(T005–011) →US2(T012–016) →US4(T017–021) →US3(T022–024) →T025–029. Within a story, failing tests precede implementation. US2/US4/US3 use the common fixture handoffs for independent story checks; their final public integration requires the earlier safe orchestration foundations. US1 is a credential-free test MVP, not a shippable unfinalized paid command. All stories and final gates are required for delivery.

[P] means distinct test files and no dependency on another task in that group: T005/T006, T017/T018. No concurrent writes to shared modules. The same implementer should sequence orchestration/adapters/bundle edits. F12 works independently; T026 is strictly serialized after its dispatch integration. Any genuinely needed shared DTO/provider amendment must be returned to the coordinator with exact owning files and justification before work, not silently included.
